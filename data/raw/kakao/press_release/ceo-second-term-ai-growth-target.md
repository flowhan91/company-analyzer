Kakao CEO Chung Shin-a won reelection for a second two-year term and announced ambitious expansion plans centered on artificial intelligence. The company established a revenue growth target exceeding 10 percent annually alongside a 10 percent operating profit margin goal for 2026.

Chung stated that "this year will mark a strategic turning point for Kakao Group to deliver growth worthy of a growth stock." The leadership team plans to streamline operations while concentrating efforts on KakaoTalk and AI development.

The company's flagship product, ChatGPT for Kakao, expanded from 2 million users at launch in October to 8 million by February. Chung explained the company's vision: "Kakao's agentic AI connects specialized agents so tasks flow naturally, resolving problems along the user's journey."

By year-end, Kakao intends to integrate external partners through PlayMCP and AI Agent Builder, positioning itself to deliver differentiated agentic AI, built on Kakao's strength in conversational context.
