South Korean technology giant Kakao announced a major restructuring that divides the company into two entities: Kakao AI and Kakao X. This strategic separation aims to accelerate decision-making and improve how capital is allocated across different business units.

Kakao AI will concentrate on artificial intelligence capabilities and the KakaoTalk messaging platform, which currently serves approximately 50 million users. The new entity plans to develop an "agentic AI interface" that understands user intentions and guides them from search through purchasing. Leadership of Kakao AI will be handled by current CEO Chung Shin-a.

Kakao X will function as an investment company overseeing financial services, entertainment, and mobility divisions, including KakaoBank, Kakao Pay, Kakao Entertainment, and Kakao Mobility. Kim Do-young, head of Kakao Investment, will lead this entity.

By 2030, the two entities target combined annual revenue exceeding 16 trillion won ($11.6 billion), with Kakao AI targeting 20 million daily active AI users, a 50% increase in time users spend on KakaoTalk, and over 1 trillion won in AI-related revenue.

Chung stated: "This separation is a decision to transform Kakao into a structure with the speed and accountability required for the AI era."

Shareholder approval is scheduled for December 17, 2026, with separation completion targeted for January 1, 2027, and relisting on January 27, 2027.
