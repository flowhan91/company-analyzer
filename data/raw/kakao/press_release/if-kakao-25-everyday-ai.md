Kakao hosted its annual if(kakao)25 conference from September 23-25 at the Kakao AI Campus in Yongin, Gyeonggi-do, under the theme "Possibility Becomes Daily Life." CEO Shina Chung announced a major transformation of KakaoTalk integrating AI services.

The company is transitioning KakaoTalk from a messaging app into an exploratory service combining artificial intelligence with everyday communication. The platform will introduce features like chat summarization, call recording, and "Kanana Search," replacing the existing search function. A new "Now tab" offers ShortForm content and community discussions.

CEO Chung stated: "The phrase 'See you on KakaoTalk' will mean experiencing a bigger world through Kakao AI."

In October, Kakao launches ChatGPT integration directly within KakaoTalk's Chats tab, powered by OpenAI's latest GPT-5 model. The "Kakao Agent" connects ChatGPT to services including Kakao Map, Melon, and Gift.

CPO Mintaek Hong emphasized: "We aimed to resolve inconveniences and make conversations more pleasant."

The proprietary "Kanana" AI model operates on-device for enhanced privacy protection. Kakao launched Korea's first "PlayMCP" platform enabling developers to create tools for AI services, with a forthcoming PlayTools marketplace.

Oliver Jay from OpenAI commented: "By combining ChatGPT with the Kakao ecosystem, AI will become even more useful in people's everyday lives."
