Kakao Corp reported second-quarter 2026 results on August 6, showing consolidated revenue of KRW 2.099 trillion, up 9% year-over-year and 8% quarter-over-quarter. Consolidated operating profit reached KRW 277 billion, up 36% year-over-year, lifting the operating margin to 13%, up roughly 3 percentage points year-over-year. Platform revenue reached KRW 1.23 trillion, up 17% year-over-year, with Talk Biz and Platform Others driving growth.

On AI strategy, management said the company's core competency is in B2C services, not infrastructure, and it will focus on the model and service layers to develop AI services that become daily habits. The company does not plan to invest in AI infrastructure directly.

By 2028, AI-related revenue is expected to account for a double-digit percentage of Talk Biz revenue, potentially pushing standalone operating margin significantly above 20%. Kakao is embedding agentic AI into KakaoTalk and daily user habits, with initial focus on food delivery via Coupang Eats and plans to expand into commerce, reservations, travel, and payments.

ChatGPT for Kakao reached 13 million cumulative subscribers with strong engagement - more than 6 daily outbound messages per user and roughly 8 minutes of daily time spent. The company targets 10 million monthly active users for AI services in KakaoTalk by year-end, with mass adoption expected by year-end and meaningful monetization in 2027.
