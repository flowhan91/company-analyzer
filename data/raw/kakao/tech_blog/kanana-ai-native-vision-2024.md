Kakao announced its transformation into an AI-native company and introduced "Kanana," an integrated AI brand representing the company's generative AI ecosystem. The announcement occurred at the if(kakaoAI)2024 developer conference in Yongin, Gyeonggi-do, which ran October 22-24 with 94 sessions.

CEO Shina Chung explained the strategic direction: "Our primary strength at Kakao lies in 'connecting relationships,' and even in the era of generative AI, we strive to provide AI services that are highly personalized."

Kanana combines "Kakao," "native," and "natural" to represent an "interactive ultra-personalized AI assistance service." The brand encompasses Kakao's language, multimodal, and visual generation models, categorized as Flag, Essence, and Nano by capacity.

Performance Lead SH Lee described the dual-function approach: Nana handles individual interactions with memory across conversations, while Kana operates within group chats, offering contextual suggestions. The company highlighted Kanana's group chat capability as differentiating it from existing AI services.

Lee stated: "Just as KakaoTalk was in the mobile era, we expect Kanana to become the easiest and most practical mainstream service for users in the generative AI era."

The launch includes a separate application distinct from KakaoTalk, with in-house testing beginning within the year.
