published_date: 2025-05-01

Kakao has announced the capabilities of its unified multimodal language model "Kanana-o" and audio language model "Kanana-a" through its official tech blog. This marks Korea's first model capable of simultaneously processing text, speech, and images in any combination.

The development utilized model merging technology to integrate "Kanana-v" (image processing) and "Kanana-a" (audio comprehension and generation). Following integration, joint training connected visual and auditory information with language capabilities. The model underwent refinement using extensive Korean datasets and can recognize regional dialects including Jeju and Gyeongsang variants.

Kanana-o demonstrates emotion recognition through analysis of intonation, speech patterns, and voice tremors. It supports streaming-based speech synthesis for minimal latency responses and achieved performance comparable to leading global models across both Korean and English benchmarks.

Kim Byeong-hak, Kakao Kanana Performance Lead, stated: "The Kanana model evolves beyond text-based AI by integrating information to see, hear, speak, and empathize like humans. We aim to strengthen AI competitiveness while contributing to Korea's AI ecosystem."

Kakao plans continued development focusing on multi-turn conversations, full-duplex capabilities, and safety measures to prevent inappropriate responses.
