NAVER Corporation presented its strategic framework for positioning artificial intelligence as essential societal infrastructure at SusHi Tech Tokyo 2026, Asia's largest technology conference held in Japan on April 27.

During the main session, CEO Choi Soo-yeon, NAVER Labs CEO Seok Sang-ok, and NAVER Cloud Executive Director Kim Ju-hee engaged in a 45-minute discussion titled "From AI to Society (Designing AI as a Social Infrastructure)."

Choi emphasized NAVER's dual strength as both a major service provider and AI developer, stating: "We aim to enhance service competitiveness and contribute to social development through Sovereign AI" grounded in understanding local users and respecting their cultures.

The company highlighted three key initiatives. CLOVA CareCall is an AI monitoring service addressing super-aging societies, currently deployed in Japanese municipalities like Izumo City for elderly resident check-ins and emergency disaster resilience. LINE WORKS is a collaboration platform featuring AI-OCR technology and voice tools like Roger, enabling digital transformation for small business owners and field workers. Digital twins and robotics technologies bridge physical and digital realms, demonstrated through projects in Saudi Arabia and Nagai City, Japan, alongside 1784, described as the world's first robot-friendly building.

Concluding remarks emphasized balancing technological scalability with social responsibility to forge meaningful connections among society, people, and technology through AI innovation.
