NAVER's Chairman Lee Hae-jin and NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang unveiled a comprehensive strategic alliance focused on building gigawatt-scale AI infrastructure. The announcement occurred during Huang's visit to NAVER's headquarters at 1784.

Infrastructure development: NAVER's GAK Sejong hyperscale data center will serve as the foundation, expanding from 55MW capacity in early 2027 to 200MW by 2028, with ultimate ambitions reaching gigawatt scale.

Technology integration: the collaboration merges NAVER's GPU cluster expertise with NVIDIA's DSX infrastructure platform. According to Huang, "we will scale this to gigawatt level, and when that vision is realized, NAVER will be a company ten times larger than it is today."

AI model collaboration: both companies will develop a "Seoul World Model" combining NVIDIA's Cosmos foundation model with NAVER's spatial intelligence capabilities. NAVER became the first Korean company joining the NVIDIA Nemotron Coalition, an alliance of 12 AI companies.

Strategic scope: this partnership extends beyond technology sharing to include joint capital participation, global market expansion across Asia-Pacific, Europe, and the Middle East, and collaborative robotics advancement.
