NAVER Cloud and NVIDIA announced a strategic partnership to develop global AI factories, combining capabilities across infrastructure, AI models, and services. The collaboration was unveiled at the NVIDIA Cloud Partner Summit in Taiwan.

NAVER Cloud CEO Kim Yu-won presented the vision at the NCP Summit, emphasizing that the company possesses "full-stack technology capabilities spanning the entire AI value chain." NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang recognized NAVER Cloud as a crucial partner in global AI infrastructure development during his keynote at NVIDIA GTC Taipei 2026.

The partnership includes technical collaboration on HyperCLOVA X using NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 Ultra language model and joint research on hyperscale language model optimization.

NAVER Cloud's Seoul World Model, built using NVIDIA Cosmos physical AI platform with 1.2 million panoramic images, demonstrates the companies' combined capabilities in physical AI and digital twin technology.

A leadership meeting was planned between NAVER's Founder Lee Haejin and NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang to detail execution plans for the global AI factory initiative.
