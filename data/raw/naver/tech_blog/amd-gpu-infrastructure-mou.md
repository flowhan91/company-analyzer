NAVER and AMD signed a Memorandum of Understanding to expand their AI ecosystem and collaborate on next-generation infrastructure. The signing ceremony took place at NAVER 1784, the company's second headquarters, with CEOs Choi Soo-yeon and Lisa Su in attendance.

The partnership focuses on building a connected AI ecosystem across diverse infrastructure environments. Key objectives include strengthening technical cooperation to create high-performance GPU computing environments optimized for HyperCLOVA X, NAVER's large language model.

NAVER brings full-stack AI capabilities spanning in-house LLMs, data centers, cloud infrastructure, and user-facing services. The company will serve as a core deployment partner for AMD's next-generation infrastructure in real-world applications.

The collaboration extends to academic support, with both companies planning to provide computing resources to researchers and pursue joint research initiatives across diverse infrastructure platforms.

NAVER CEO Choi Soo-yeon stated: "This collaboration marks a meaningful step toward securing technological diversity and strengthening our AI infrastructure competitiveness." AMD CEO Lisa Su commented that NAVER is the ideal partner to innovatively implement AMD's next-generation AI GPU technology, emphasizing building an open AI ecosystem trusted by researchers, enterprises, and developers.
