NAVER Corporation announced Q2 2026 financial results showing revenue growth of 16.2% year-over-year, reaching KRW 3.3888 trillion, with operating profit at KRW 520.3 billion. The company attributed this performance to strong results in advertising and commerce, supported by expanded AI integration. The decline in operating profit reflected one-time costs related to the rollout of its offline integrated payment terminal, Npay Connect, broadcasting rights for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, intellectual property recognition expenses, and investment in GPU-based AI infrastructure.

Key business segment performance: NAVER Platform revenue reached KRW 1.9022 trillion, up 12.3% year-over-year. Financial Platform reached KRW 470.7 billion, up 16.0% year-over-year. Global Initiatives reached KRW 1.0159 trillion, up 24.4% year-over-year.

Advertising revenue increased 7.5% year-over-year, with AI contributing more than 60% of the growth. The newly launched AI Briefing ads showed click conversion rates more than 30% higher and purchase conversion rates more than three times those of existing Search Ads.

Global C2C revenue surged 74.9% year-over-year, driven by performance at Wallapop, Poshmark, and SODA. Npay payment volume reached KRW 25.2 trillion, up 21.0% year-over-year.

CEO Choi Soo-yeon highlighted the strategic partnership with NVIDIA: "Through NVIDIA's ecosystem and our enterprise and public-sector customer base, we have already secured customers for our initial operating capacity."
