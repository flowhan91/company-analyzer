NAVER outlined its strategy to integrate artificial intelligence with its established content ecosystem. The company plans to invest 1 trillion Korean won over five years to strengthen creator relationships and content quality in the AI era.

The company is launching "NAVER Mate," a fellowship program recognizing approximately 3,000 creators monthly. As Kim Kwang-hyun, Chief Data & Contents Officer, explained: "Platform competitiveness in the AI era will be determined by technology that discovers quality creators" and user recognition of their work.

AI Briefing reaches 30 million monthly users, while AI Tab exceeded 3 million cumulative users within its first month of beta testing. A new Smart Lens version launches June 2026, enabling photo-based information retrieval connected to actionable services.

The company emphasizes three core competitive advantages: a proprietary language model optimized for service scenarios, approximately 10 billion data records with API tools, and operational engineering expertise. The next-generation HyperCLOVA X model will enhance these services.
