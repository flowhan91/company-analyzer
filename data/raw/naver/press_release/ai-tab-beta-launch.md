published_date: 2026-04-28

NAVER Corporation announced the beta launch of AI Tab, its conversational AI search service, for NAVER Plus Membership users on April 27, 2026. The company intends to refine the service based on feedback from its engaged membership base before expanding to all users during the first half of 2026.

AI Tab processes a broad range of queries, from everyday questions to complex research requests. The system understands context and search intent, allowing users to refine results through dialogue. Access points include the main search bar on NAVER's PC homepage, AI Briefings, and integrated search results across Shopping and SmartPlace platforms.

The service integrates NAVER's ecosystem, including Shopping, SmartPlace, Blogs, and Cafes, to deliver unified information. For example, when users request local recommendations, the system combines venue data with user-generated reviews to provide comprehensive, actionable insights on a single screen.

CEO Choi Soo-yeon noted that AI Tab marks a turning point in search, evolving from exploration toward execution. Future enhancements include Smart Lens integration for multimodal search and proactive query suggestions.
