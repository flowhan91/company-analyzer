NAVER has deployed AI Tab as a fully available conversational search feature for its user base. The service replaces the Green Dot icon on NAVER's mobile search interface, positioning Smart Lens as a prominent multimodal search option alongside it.

During a two-month beta period, AI Tab accumulated over 4 million users. Performance metrics showed click-through rates exceeding 20% for product and location cards. Users engaging frequently with the service, 11 or more visits, generated 2.7 times more product clicks and double the location clicks compared to infrequent users.

The platform operates on a specialized large-scale language model optimized for NAVER's environment, which processes approximately 50 million daily visitors. The system integrates real-time map data and reservation availability directly into search responses.

Future expansion plans include real estate and health-focused agent features launching later in 2026, plus integration with NAVER's Whale browser. According to Kim Kwang-hyun, Chief Data & Contents Officer: "AI Tab brings together NAVER's service ecosystem, data infrastructure and AI capabilities," positioning the tool as an "action-oriented AI agent" connecting discovery to tangible user actions.
