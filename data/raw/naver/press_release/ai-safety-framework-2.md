NAVER Corp. (CEO Choi Soo-yeon) introduced ASF 2.0, an updated framework for managing artificial intelligence safety, at the Seoul Forum on AI Safety & Security (SFASS) held in Gangnam, Seoul, on July 8. The new version builds upon the original framework presented at the 2024 AI Seoul Summit and focuses on protecting users experiencing AI services, not just evaluating individual model performance.

The evolution represents a shift in approach. Rather than concentrating solely on individual model performance, ASF 2.0 addresses how multiple AI systems work together in real-world applications. Song Dae-seop, NAVER's AI Safety Policy leader, noted: "the challenge is no longer simply how to make a single model safe, but how to safely design and operate multi-model services."

Key components of ASF 2.0 include an AI Risk Taxonomy that categorizes potential dangers and an AI Impact Assessment Matrix evaluating anticipated consequences. The framework also introduces CHEC 2.0, a companywide execution system ensuring consistent application throughout service development.

NAVER applied ASF 2.0 to its AI Tab service, conducting safety reviews across the entire lifecycle from design through launch. The company intends to extend this methodology to additional AI-powered services currently in development and already operational.
