NAVER Corporation reported first-quarter 2026 financial results demonstrating substantial growth across its business segments. The company achieved consolidated revenue of KRW 3.2411 trillion, representing a 16.3% year-on-year increase, alongside operating profit of KRW 541.8 billion, up 7.2% compared to the prior year.

The performance was driven by artificial intelligence integration within core business operations and accelerated expansion in the consumer-to-consumer sector. CEO Choi Soo-yeon stated: "NAVER is an unparalleled platform that seamlessly integrates search, commerce, and payment infrastructure" for the AI era.

Revenue by segment: NAVER Platform reached KRW 1.8398 trillion, up 14.7% year-on-year. Financial Platform reached KRW 459.7 billion, up 18.9% year-on-year. Global Initiatives reached KRW 941.6 billion, up 18.4% year-on-year.

The company restructured its revenue classification system starting Q1 2026 to better reflect core operations and emerging business opportunities. The NAVER Platform segment encompasses advertising and services, while Global Initiatives includes C2C marketplaces, content divisions, and enterprise solutions.

C2C marketplace growth, including KREAM, SODA, Poshmark, and Wallapop, substantially contributed to Global Initiatives performance, increasing 57.7% year-on-year.
